A made-up company

Meet Veridian Life Sciences, the customer in every demo.

Veridian makes medicines, vaccines and consumer health products. It is not a real company. I made it up so I can show agents working on something shaped like a large regulated business, without using anyone's real network.

In the story, Netwik is the outside firm that runs Veridian's network. Netwik itself is a personal project, not a company.
