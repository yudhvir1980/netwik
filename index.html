Agents need context.

AI models get smarter every day. What they do not have is the context of your network. Without it, an agent is a brilliant new starter on its first day, every day.

The context layer turns your knowledge, expertise and processes into context a machine can use.

Knowledge

What exists. Sites, devices, circuits, and what depends on what.

Expertise

How the network is managed. What your engineers learned from outages and failed changes.

Processes

Which actions are acceptable, and who has to approve them.

Run like software: owned, approved, versioned and kept current.
