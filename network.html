The network the agents work on: 755 devices.

Veridian's network is made up, but it is really there: every device is a record in a ServiceNow instance that the agents read. The numbers on this page were counted from that instance on , not taken from a design.

The design covers all 31 sites. What is loaded today adds up to five of them: a data centre, the London head office, the Newcastle and Pune plants, and one sales office.

Every device, one square each
